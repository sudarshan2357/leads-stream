# Privacy Policy

**App:** Lead Stream Demo | **Last updated:** 11 October 2026

Lead Stream Demo is a student demonstration project, not a commercial product.

**What we collect:** the name, email address, phone number and any other
answers submitted through a Facebook Lead Ad test form connected to our Page.
Most submissions are test leads with placeholder data.

**How we use it:** only to display the submission live on a demo mobile screen
and to test the integration. It is not used for marketing, advertising or
profiling.

**Storage and sharing:** data is stored in a database used only for this demo.
We do not sell or share it with anyone. Meta delivers the submissions to us.
We do not use cookies or analytics.

**Retention:** data is deleted after the demonstration.

## Data Deletion

To request deletion of your data, email **rishabhsingh2537@gmail.com** with the
subject "Data deletion request" and the name, email or phone number you entered
in the form. We will delete the matching records within 7 days and confirm by
email.
